Promising pilot, incomplete acceptance evidence

Only 0.2 s of source traffic: about 20 small messages or six images per camera. Stereo is 640×480 RGB at 30 Hz. These runs do not establish sustained throughput or stable latency tails.

Latest-value policies remain active. In the mixed image workload, the rewrite received 46/50 joint updates, 17/51 control commands and 16/200 clock updates. Lower delay among surviving messages alone cannot establish success.
