Interpretation

Each timing window has a 0.2 s source period: about 50 joint/effort and 200 clock receipts. Joint/effort p99 therefore equals the observed maximum. Repetitions describe variability; these tests do not establish sustainable throughput or a tail guarantee.

Publish-entry latency includes synchronous publication, bridge delivery, reception and the wait for callback execution. Payloads are preallocated and reused: actual sample creation/capture is not separately timestamped. Scheduled-release age also includes source scheduling delay. Neither is relabeled as creation-to-callback latency.
