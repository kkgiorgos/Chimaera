How to read throughput

Rates count unique receipts from a finite source cohort, including arrivals during drain, divided by the source-window duration. They show delivered load, with payload headers excluded. No saturation sweep has established maximum capacity. Stereo RGB payload at full delivery is 55.296 MB per source-second (two 921,600-byte images × 30 Hz).

One repeat per case; 60 stereo pairs in final tests and 6 frames per eye in pilots. Historical pilots use older DDS settings and endpoints, so cross-cohort percentages would confound implementation and benchmark changes. The native gap also includes modeled guest CPU/memory costs.
